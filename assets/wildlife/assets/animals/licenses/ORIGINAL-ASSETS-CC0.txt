Original Terrain Lab procedural animal assets

The koi, deer, wolf, rabbit and shark geometry and animation data were generated
specifically for this prototype. They contain no third-party model geometry,
textures, or motion-capture data. To the extent copyright applies to these
original generated assets, they are dedicated to the public domain under CC0
1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

This dedication covers the five original GLB assets, not the third-party fox,
Three.js, or the existing Terrain Lab application.
